Everything you're wondering.

How much does a website cost in Nigeria?

At Pejji, from ₦60k for a 48-hour digital business card, ₦150k for a proper small-business site, ₦300k for a conversion-focused Growth build, and ₦800k for a full shoppable store. Each pairs with a monthly Care Plan (from ₦15k/mo) that keeps it run, secured and growing. Every price is published. No quote games.

Do I really need a website if I already sell on WhatsApp and social media?

Social media is rented land; a website is owned. Over 80% of Nigerian SMEs have no website, which means the ones that do are capturing the customers you're missing. A site shows up on Google, builds trust on sight, and turns visits into WhatsApp conversations that close, even while you sleep.

What exactly does the monthly Care Plan cover?

It is not hosting. It is a team on shift: managed content changes on request, Securva security monitoring and audits, SEO tune-ups, WhatsApp and Paystack kept healthy, hosting with SSL and daily backups, and a plain-English monthly report. You message us; we handle it.

How is Pejji different on security?

Our security arm, Securva, finds and responsibly discloses real vulnerabilities in widely used software, with published CVE credits (Gitea, File Browser). Every Pejji build passes those same checks before launch and stays under watch on the Care Plan: hardened headers, secure forms, safe Paystack payments, NDPA-compliant data handling.

How fast can I be live?

Card builds go live in 48 hours. Larger builds take longer because they carry more, but every project starts with a short WhatsApp brief and ends with a link on your own domain. You review a live preview on your phone before anything ships.

What is the Pro Max AI agent?

A custom AI agent built on your site, your WhatsApp and your data. It answers customers, takes bookings and orders, follows up on quotes, and reports to you every morning. From ₦1.5M plus monthly, scoped per business on a call.
